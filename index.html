Srokstoungis Admin — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

1. Who we are

Srokstoungis Admin (“the app”) is an internal administration application provided by Srokstoung International School (“the School,” “we,” “us”). It is used only by the School’s authorized administrators and staff. Accounts are created and managed by the School; there is no public sign-up, and the app is not intended for students, parents, or the general public.

2. Information the app handles

  • Account & authentication: your administrator ID and authentication tokens, used to sign you in and keep your session secure.
  • School records you access: to perform administrative work, the app displays and lets you manage records held by the School, which may include student and guardian names, contact details, academic placement, fee/finance status, health or special-needs notes, and photos. This information belongs to the School and is processed on the School’s behalf.
  • Camera & photos: with your permission, the app uses the camera and photo library solely to capture or attach student profile/ID photos within authorized workflows.
  • Technical data: standard information required to communicate securely with the School’s servers.

3. How we use information

Information is used solely to provide administrative functionality — dashboards, staff messaging, and student-records management. We do not use it for advertising, and the app contains no third-party advertising or tracking technologies.

4. Sharing

Information is transmitted only between the app and the School’s own backend servers over encrypted HTTPS connections. We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

5. Storage & security

Authentication tokens are stored in the device’s secure keychain/keystore and are removed on sign-out. All network communication uses HTTPS. Records are stored on the School’s servers under the School’s control.

6. Children’s information

The app is used by adult staff, not by children. It processes information about students — who may be minors — strictly for the School’s legitimate educational and administrative purposes, with the School acting as the data controller.

7. Data retention & your rights

Records are retained by the School according to its policies. Because the School is the data controller, requests regarding personal data (access, correction, or deletion) should be directed to the School using the contact details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the “last updated” date above will change accordingly.
