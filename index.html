Privacy Policy - Srokstoungis Admin

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Srokstoungis Admin (the "App") is the official administration application of Srokstoung International School ("SSIS", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how that information is used, and how it is protected.

The App is provided for authorized school staff and administrators only. It is not intended for use by students, parents, or the general public.

1. Who can use the App

Access requires an SSIS staff account that is created and provided by the school. The App does not offer public registration or self sign-up. Only adult staff members (18 years or older) authorized by the school may use the App.

2. Information we collect

When you use the App, we collect and process the following information, which is transmitted securely to the school's own backend systems:

  • Your account and identity information - your name, staff user ID, email address, and phone number, used to sign you in and manage your account.
  • Student records you view and manage - to carry out school administration, the App displays and lets authorized staff manage student information held by the school. This may include a student's name, date of birth, place of birth, nationality, gender, religion, contact details (address, phone number, and email), guardian details, academic placement (program, level, and class), photographs, and health-related information such as allergies, medical conditions, blood type, and special-needs notes.
  • Messages - messages you send and receive through the App's staff messaging feature.

The App does not collect your location, does not contain advertising, and does not use third-party analytics or tracking technologies.

3. How we use information

We use the information only to operate the App and support school administration, including to:

  • authenticate your account and keep your session secure;
  • show dashboards and student information relevant to your role;
  • allow authorized staff to manage student records and photographs;
  • enable staff-to-staff communication within the school.

4. Student and children's information

The App is a tool used by adult staff to administer the school. In the course of this work, staff may handle information about students, who may be minors. This can include sensitive information such as religion and health or special-needs details, along with names, dates of birth, contact details, guardian details, academic placement, and photographs. This information is provided by, and processed on behalf of, Srokstoung International School, which acts as the controller of student data. It is used only for legitimate school administration, is accessible only to authorized staff, and is transmitted over encrypted connections.

5. How information is shared

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing. Information is transmitted only to the school's own secure backend systems operated for SSIS. We may disclose information where we are required to do so by law.

6. Data security

  • All data exchanged between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored securely on your device using the platform's secure storage.
  • Access to the App and its data is restricted to authenticated, authorized staff accounts.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain account and school records for as long as necessary to operate the school's administration systems. When a staff member leaves or no longer requires access, their account is deactivated rather than deleted, and associated records are retained in accordance with the school's record-keeping requirements. Because the App does not allow self-service account creation, it does not provide in-app account deletion. To ask a question about your data or request a change, please contact us using the details below.

8. Permissions the App uses

  • Camera and photos - to capture or select student photographs for upload.
  • Internet / network access - to communicate with the school's backend systems.
  • Notifications - to alert you to relevant activity, where this feature is enabled.

9. Your choices and rights

If you have questions about the information the App processes about you, or you wish to request access to or correction of that information, please contact us using the details below. Requests relating to student information should be directed to the school administration.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material change will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
